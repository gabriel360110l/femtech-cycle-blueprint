# Visual Direction

## Color
- Main accent: soft terracotta / dusty rose
- Background: warm off-white / cream
- Avoid bright pink and strong green as primary colors

## Central element
- Stylized, simplified uterus illustration (not realistic anatomy)
- Changes subtly per phase (color, lining thickness, soft glow at ovulation)

## Overall feel
- Calm, warm, adult
- Lots of whitespace
- Easy to scan
- Not childish, not overly medical
