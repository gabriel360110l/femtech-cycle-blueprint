# European Standards & Guidelines (Not Only US)

This app should feel European. Prefer European clinical sources, terminology and privacy expectations (GDPR).

## Primary European clinical references

| Organisation | Focus | Why relevant for the app | Link |
|--------------|-------|--------------------------|------|
| **NVOG** (NL) | Gynaecology guidelines | Dutch standard for PCOS, amenorrhoea, bleeding, fertility | https://www.nvog.nl/richtlijnen |
| **NHG** (NL) | GP guidelines | House-doctor language for exports (Amenorroe, Vaginaal bloedverlies, Subfertiliteit) | https://richtlijnen.nhg.org |
| **KNOV** (NL) | Midwifery | Pregnancy and post-partum practice in the Netherlands | https://www.knov.nl |

| **Voedingscentrum / RIVM** (NL) | Nutrition in pregnancy | Official Dutch advice on food safety, vitamins, caffeine | https://www.voedingscentrum.nl |
| **Lareb** (NL) | Medication in pregnancy | Teratology information centre | https://www.lareb.nl |

## Practical implications for the app

- Use **Dutch/European terminology** in the doctor export (not American terms).
- Prefer **Sensiplan** (German-developed, widely used in Europe) over purely US methods (e.g. TCOYF) as the primary symptothermal engine.
- Align fertile-window and prediction language with European fertility guidelines (ESHRE / NICE / NVOG).
- GDPR by design: local-first, clear consent, easy data export and deletion.
- Units: metric (°C, ml, kg, cm).
- Support for European languages (NL, EN, DE, FR at minimum for later).
