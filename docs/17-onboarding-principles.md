# Onboarding Principles

Keep it short. People should be able to start tracking within 1 minute.

## Maximum at first launch
1. What do you want to track? (Cycle / Trying to conceive / Just period)
2. Average cycle length (optional, can skip)
3. Do you want to log temperature? (yes/no)

Everything else comes later, optionally.

## Rules
- No long questionnaires
- No forced account creation
- Allow skip on almost everything
- Explain why a question is asked (one short sentence)
